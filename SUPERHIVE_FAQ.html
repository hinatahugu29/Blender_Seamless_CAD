Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Which operating systems are supported?

A: Currently, Blender CAD supports Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Support for macOS and Linux is in active development and planned for future updates.

Q2: Which Blender versions are compatible?

A: Blender CAD is compatible with Blender 4.2 LTS to Blender 5.1+.

Q3: Can I export models to CAD software like SolidWorks or Fusion 360?

A: Yes! You can export true solid geometry directly as STEP and IGES files, making them ready for SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Rhino, FreeCAD, or manufacturing/3D printing workflows.

Q4: Do I need separate CAD software or licenses installed?

A: No. Blender CAD incorporates an integrated CAD kernel (OpenCASCADE) directly into Blender. You do not need any third-party CAD licenses.

Q5: Are future updates included with my purchase?

A: Yes! All updates for the 8.x series are included free of charge.

Q6: How do I report a bug or request a feature?

A: You can contact us via our support email or Superhive messaging. We regularly publish updates based on user feedback!
