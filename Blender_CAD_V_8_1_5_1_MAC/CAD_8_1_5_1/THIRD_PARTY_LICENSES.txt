1. Open CASCADE Technology (OCCT) 8.0.0

             with the Open CASCADE exception version 1.0

    Full text: licenses/OCCT_LICENSE_LGPL_21.txt
               licenses/OCCT_LGPL_EXCEPTION.txt

    OCCT is the geometry kernel. Seamless CAD ships it as shared libraries
    (.dll on Windows, .so on Linux, .dylib on macOS) alongside the
    cad_server executable, and links against them dynamically. The
    libraries are unmodified.

    NOTICE, as required by the Open CASCADE exception version 1.0:

        This software makes use of and is based on facilities provided by
        the Open CASCADE Technology software.

    Under LGPL-2.1 you are entitled to replace the bundled OCCT libraries
    with your own build. They live next to the cad_server executable in
    the add-on directory; replacing them there is sufficient, as no OCCT
    code is statically linked into cad_server.

4. Rust crates
--------------

    The cad_server executable is built in Rust and statically links a
    number of crates, all of them under permissive licenses (MIT and/or
    Apache-2.0, and in some cases the Unicode licence or BSD variants).

    To reproduce the exact list for a given build:

        cd src_rust
        cargo install cargo-about
        cargo about generate about.hbs

    Notable direct dependencies: wgpu, rayon, serde, serde_json,
    bytemuck, memmap2, pollster, once_cell, cpp, ezpz.
