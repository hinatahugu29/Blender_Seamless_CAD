Blender CAD: Industrial-Grade Solid Modeling Inside Blender

Transform Blender into a true CAD workstation. Powered by the OpenCASCADE technology, Blender CAD brings precision solid geometry, non-destructive CAD Booleans, smooth fillets, and native STEP/IGES file exchange directly into your Blender 3D viewport.

✨ Key Features

1. Industrial CAD Kernel Engine

  • OpenCASCADE Integration: Construct true solid geometry without topology artifacts, polygon stretching, or shading glitches.
  • High Precision: Experience mathematical accuracy for mechanical engineering, product design, and 3D printing.

2. Advanced CAD Booleans & Fillets

  • Flawless Booleans: Union, Difference, and Intersection operations that work seamlessly on complex shapes where traditional mesh Booleans fail.
  • Variable & Edge Fillets: Create perfect, smooth CAD fillets and chamfers effortlessly.

3. Seamless Industry Interoperability (STEP / IGES)

  • Direct STEP & IGES Import/Export: Bridge the gap between Blender and professional CAD software like SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Rhino, and FreeCAD.
  • Manufacturing Ready: Export production-grade CAD files ready for CNC machining, mold design, or additive manufacturing.

4. Native Blender Integration

  • Intuitive UI: Built to feel like a native part of Blender. No steep learning curve.
  • Non-Blocking Performance: Fast, multithreaded calculation powered by C++/Rust core backend.
